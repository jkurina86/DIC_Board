## Active-device and pin-function coverage

**TPS27S100APWPR U30,U31,U33,U35,U36,U37,U38:** all seven PWP14 symbols have input8/9/10, output5/6/7, ground2/EP15, enable3, diagnostic-enable12 grounded, ILIM13 to1k/ground, FLT14 unconnected, NC1/4/11 unconnected. This matches TI pin table p3. Rails12,6.6,5V meet3.5–40V operating range. Footprint belongs to HTSSOP14 PWP family; PCB thermal copper/vias cannot be assessed. Internal limit and thermal cycling protect the IC but do not demonstrate protection of each motor. Every channel defaults off through100k EN pulldown. Where15k series is fitted (R309,R312,R315,R340), EN=3.3×100/(100+15)=2.870V nominal, above2V VIH, with external-current/noise/protection tradeoff. Even a2.9V MCU high leavesabout2.52V before input leakage. Direct-enable U30/U31/U33 are acceptable logic-level-wise. Shared-ground external faults remain a system consideration.

All seven1k ILIM resistors give `Ilim=2000×1.233V/1000Ω=2.466A` nominal; datasheet accuracy at this range is±15% above25°C and±18% below25°C, plus resistor tolerance. No claim that22nF output caps clamp transients: TPS27S100 itself has stated IN/OUT IEC transient protection; external signal lines have separate protection requirements.
