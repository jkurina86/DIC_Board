# 12 V regulator replacement — 16 September 2026

The 12 V schematic now uses TPS552892RYQR for the user-confirmed 10–14 V input range (12 V nominal). The design objective is 12 V / 3 A continuous; 4 A operation remains subject to thermal and transient qualification. The 5 A protection setting is not a continuous-output rating.

## Implemented changes

- U10: TPS552882RPMR → TPS552892RYQR. Created project-local `DIC_Board:TPS552892` symbol and `DIC_Board:Texas_RYQ0021A_TPS552892` footprint. All 21 pin numbers/functions were checked against TI's pin table. The footprint follows RYQ0021A drawing 4226658/A, including five separate long power pads and segmented paste. Pad 9 is PGND; pads 7, 8, 10 and 11 must not be joined to ground.
- Removed Q100/Q101, gate resistors R114/R115, and the old inductor-limit resistor R113. The new regulator has its four MOSFETs and inductor-current limit internally.
- Retained L10 = 4.7 µH, R102 = 49.9 kΩ (approximately 395 kHz), input/output bypass capacitors, and 100 nF bootstrap capacitors.
- R103 = 10 mΩ, Vishay WSL2512R0100FEA, 1%, 1 W. Nominal output limit 5 A; threshold/resistor tolerance gives approximately 4.75–5.26 A before thermal drift. Feedback senses after R103. At 3 A its nominal dissipation is 90 mW; at 5 A, 250 mW.
- R104/R105 remain 100 kΩ / 11.1 kΩ: 12.0108 V nominal. Retained the existing 150 kΩ / 24.9 kΩ EN divider, approximately 8.64 V rising and 7.89 V falling; this is a brownout threshold, not an expansion of the specified 10–14 V operating range.
- R112 now pulls MODE low for automatic PFM at light load. EXTVCC is intentionally open to select internal bias. PG, CC and CDC are unused. Added a post-shunt power flag to describe the actual supply path through R103.
- C204/C214/C224 changed from 330 µF to 100 µF, Nichicon UHE1H101MPD, 50 V, ±20%, 8 × 11.5 mm radial body with 3.5 mm lead pitch. Their footprint assignments were updated to match. This is the only change to the valve sheets; no valve wiring or driver settings changed.
- TP22 retains `TestPoint:TestPoint_Pad_D1.0mm`. All cross-sheet interfaces and load supply voltages remain unchanged.

## Capacitance and startup

Fresh hierarchical netlist: 308.1 µF nominal before R103, 401 µF after R103, for **709.1 µF total** directly connected to the 12 V output. A +20% capacitance bound gives **850.92 µF**, below the regulator's listed 1000 µF effective-capacitance ceiling. DC bias reduces ceramic capacitance; the minimum effective values still require part-specific assessment.

Charging 850.92 µF to the conservative upper output estimate of 12.35 V in 2.5 ms requires 4.204 A average, below the 4.752 A calculated minimum shunt limit. This is a charge-budget screening calculation, not a simulated startup waveform. Keep downstream converters and switched loads disabled until the rail settles. Sequence pump and actuator starts. Added off-board capacitance and premature load enabling consume the remaining margin.

Reducing valve bulk does not authorize extra simultaneous valves or arbitrary pulse timing. A 100 µF capacitor with 0.6 V allowed droop supplies one nominal 104 mA coil for only about 0.58 ms; the converter must supply the sustained pulse. Check remote-bank droop with the final interconnect and driver switching pattern.

## Compensation and thermal screening

Retained R106 = 10 kΩ, C104 = 100 nF, C105 = 1 nF. An analytical average-current model using TI equations 19–26 was screened over 648 combinations: 10/12/14 V input; 11.68/12.35 V output; 0.3/3/4 A loads; 200/450/851 µF effective output capacitance; 0/20/100 mΩ equivalent ESR; transconductance ±20% sensitivity; and compensation-capacitance factors 0.765/1.265. A one-cycle transport delay was included. Minimum calculated phase margin was 64.1°, minimum finite gain margin 18.5 dB, and maximum crossover 3.63 kHz.

These are **screening results only**. The model uses lumped capacitance, a resistive load, an ideal inner current loop, and a D=0 buck proxy. It excludes distributed output impedances, downstream converters' constant-power dynamics, PFM behavior, switching transitions, and PCB parasitics. No SPICE simulator was available. This is not hardware stability signoff.

At 10 V input, 12 V / 3 A output and assumed 90% efficiency, average input/inductor current is 4 A. Nominal ripple is approximately 0.90 A peak-to-peak at the selected frequency, with about 4.45 A peak current. The retained XAL1010-472MEB has substantial current margin. Layout, hot inductance, core loss, and regulator junction temperature still require qualification. Integration concentrates MOSFET heat in U10, so do not infer thermal adequacy from the removed discrete MOSFET footprints.
