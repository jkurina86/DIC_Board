# DMC3016LDV-13 library model

Imported from the user-provided `LIB_DMC3016LDV-13.zip` (SamacSys / Component Search Engine) on 2026-09-10.
Source: https://componentsearchengine.com/part-view/DMC3016LDV-13/Diodes%20Incorporated
Datasheet: https://www.diodes.com/datasheet/download/DMC3016LDV.pdf (DS38936 Rev. 1-2, page 1).

The supplied legacy KiCad symbol was converted with KiCad 10. Its single-unit graphics and all ten pins are preserved. Project MPN, Manufacturer, and Mouser properties were added; its footprint and datasheet links were resolved.

The associated `DMT3020LDV13` footprint and STEP model came from the same archive. Exposed pad numbers 9 and 10 were swapped to correct a source-model error: pad 9 (D1) belongs behind pins 1/2 and 7/8, at negative X; pad 10 (D2) belongs behind pins 3/4 and 5/6, at positive X. These extra pad numbers are CAD conventions for the exposed drains, not additional package leads. Footprint dimensions and the 3D model have not undergone a full fabrication review.
